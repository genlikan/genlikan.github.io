I'm Jacob Yoon Zeng Yew, but some people call me Gen.

### What I do

I'm the founding Forward Deployed AI Engineer at [StackAI](https://www.stack-ai.com). I started by taking customer AI workflows from first call to production, built out our integrations program, and now lead the Forward Deployed Engineering team.

### Before that

I studied Information Technology at the University of Massachusetts Boston, majoring in Information Architecture. My final project was a [3D schedule visualizer](https://genlikan.github.io/cs460student/FinalProject/CS460_Final_Project_Report_genlikan.pdf) built with WebGL.

When COVID hit, I took a temporary data-entry job at MIT Sloan. I automated months of that work in about two hours, which turned into an IT platform role there and later IT consulting for Harvard Medical School. Around then I got an early look at what language models could do and became convinced they were the future, so I went back to school for a Master's in Data Science at Wentworth Institute of Technology, focused on AI/ML. I spent it on machine learning, reinforcement learning and building with large language models, then returned to MIT Sloan as an AI/ML engineer before joining StackAI.

### Outside work

Games, video editing, voice acting and the occasional beatbox. I speak English, Mandarin Chinese and Bahasa Melayu.
